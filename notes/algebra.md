# Algebra

## Polynomial rings

Let \(k\) be a field. The polynomial ring in \(n\) variables is

$$
R = k[x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n].
$$

An ideal generated by \(f_1,\ldots,f_m\) is

$$
I = (f_1, f_2, \ldots, f_m).
$$
